1. Overview

Guess the World is a free quiz game with four endless modes: Guess the Country, Guess the Flag, Geography Quiz, and Guess the City. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, uses, and shares.

2. Information We Collect

The app itself does not collect any personal information. It does not ask you to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, email address, phone number, location, photos, or contacts. Your game progress and scores are stored only on your own device.

3. Advertising — Google AdMob

Guess the World shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and share the following information for advertising purposes:

  • Advertising ID (Google Advertising ID)
  • Device information, such as device model and operating system version
  • Approximate location and IP address, as used by AdMob to serve ads

AdMob may use this information to serve ads and measure their performance. You can reset or opt out of personalised advertising in your Android device settings under Settings > Privacy > Ads. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy and Google Play Services at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

4. How We Use Information

Information collected by AdMob is used only to display ads and to measure ad performance. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use advertising data for any other purpose.

5. Data Sharing

Data is shared only with Google / AdMob for advertising, as described above. We do not share data with any other third party.

6. Children

Guess the World is not directed at children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

The app itself stores no personal data on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete. Advertising data held by Google is governed by Google's own retention policies. To delete the app and all data stored on your device, uninstall Guess the World from your device.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted at this same address, with a new “Last updated” date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at FruitLearningGames20069@gmail.com.
